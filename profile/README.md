# Templates for An Artificial Life CS Class

The templates here correspond to labs and assignments for the class CS 361: Artificial Life and Digital Evolution at Carleton College. 
[Syllabus](https://anyaevostinar.github.io/classes/361-f26/syllabus) and [Schedule](https://anyaevostinar.github.io/classes/361-f26/schedule) are publicly available and the schedule includes links to the homework and lab prompts along with accompanying readings and slides.
